About Ace Tailors

Austin's oldest tailor shop

Ace Tailors is Austin's oldest tailor shop, run by owner Vanessa Wilkerson. Generations of Austinites have trusted the shop with their best clothes — and their tightest deadlines.

Express turnaround, leather alterations and major resizing are the specialities, alongside everyday alterations and suit tailoring.
