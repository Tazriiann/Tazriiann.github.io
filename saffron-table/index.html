Welcome to Saffron Table

One spice. Endless seasons.

Every dish at Saffron Table begins the same way — with a pinch of saffron, bloomed in warm water until it turns the colour of late-evening sun. From there, the seasons decide the rest.

Chef Amara Sethi and her small team cook a menu that changes with the market: forty seats, two sittings a night, and food that is generous first and clever second.
