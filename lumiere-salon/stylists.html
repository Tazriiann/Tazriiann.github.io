The team

Book the person, not just the appointment

Four specialists who each do one thing brilliantly. Read their notes, pick your person, and they'll be expecting you.

Isabelle Moreau

Creative Director & Master Colourist · 14 years

Isabelle trained in Paris and London before founding Lumière in 2014. She's the salon's colour brain — balayage that grows out like it was always yours, and cuts designed around how your hair moves, not how it photographs for one day. Her consultations are famously honest: if a colour will wreck your hair, she'll tell you, then build you a better plan.

  • Balayage
  • Precision cuts
  • Colour correction
  • Editorial styling
Book with Isabelle

Amara Osei

Bridal & Makeup Lead · 9 years

Amara has styled more than 200 wedding mornings and has never once been the stressful part of one. She leads our bridal and occasion work — sculpted updos, soft-glam makeup that survives happy tears, and a timeline she runs like a friendly train schedule. Trials happen at the salon with daylight, honesty and very good coffee.

  • Bridal hair
  • Occasion makeup
  • Updos
  • Party styling
Book with Amara

Sofia Andersson

Skin & Spa Therapist · 8 years

Sofia runs the quiet end of the salon: two treatment rooms, low light, and facials built on real skin analysis rather than a menu script. She keeps notes on every guest's skin, so your third facial is better than your first. Her hot stone massage has a waitlist on Saturdays for a reason.

  • Signature facials
  • Hot stone massage
  • Aromatherapy
  • Skin programmes
Book with Sofia

Lena Petrova

Nail Artistry Lead · 7 years

Lena's station is where nail art stops being a photo you saved and starts being yours. Gel finishes that genuinely last, hand-painted detail done freehand, and extensions that look like nails rather than equipment. Bring a wild idea — she likes those best.

  • Gel art
  • Extensions
  • Spa pedicure
  • Hand-painted detail
Book with Lena
